> Explain this regex: ^v?(\d+)\.(\d+)\.(\d+)(-[0-9A-Za-z.]+)?$

● It matches a version number such as v2.10.3 or 1.0.0-rc.1:
  ^v?          an optional v at the start
  (\d+)\.      the major number, then a dot
  (\d+)\.      the minor number, then a dot
  (\d+)        the patch number
  (-[0-9A-Za-z.]+)?   an optional pre-release tag, like -rc.1
  $            nothing after it
